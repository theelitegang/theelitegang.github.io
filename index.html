Guidelines

Server Rules

Simple rules to keep things chill. Break 'em and you're out.

1

No Griefing or Stealing

Don't touch what isn't yours. Unclaimed builds are still off-limits — use the claiming system.

2

No Hacking or Exploiting

X-ray, fly hacks, speed hacks, duping — all bannable. If you find a bug, report it.

3

No Spamming

No spamming in chat or VCs unless it's an absolute emergency.

4

Banned Words

No saying '67' (and any of its forms), any Italian brainrot, mango(es), mustard, sprunki, or labubu.

5

No AFK in VCs

Do not sit AFK in voice channels unless you are deafened.

6

Immediate Ban Enforcement

Breaking any of the rules above will result in an immediate ban. No warnings.
